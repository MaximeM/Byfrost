Questions,
simply answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. Download Byfrost on your devices and start using it. Friends you share with don't even need an account.

Do I need a fast internet connection?

At home, your Wi-Fi is enough. Away from home, a normal broadband or 5G connection works for apps; games feel best on a good connection.

Is it safe?

Your devices talk to each other directly and everything is encrypted. Sharing links expire, and guests can only watch until you give them control.

Which devices does it work on?

Mac and Windows computers, plus iPhone and iPad to use them from anywhere. Linux is on its way.
