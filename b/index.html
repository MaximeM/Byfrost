Built for speed, from the GPU up.

Written in Rust, with hardware doing the heavy work on both ends.

  • Hardware encoding

    VideoToolbox on Mac, NVENC and D3D12 on Windows.

  • Peer to peer

    Your machines connect to each other directly whenever the network allows it.

  • Encrypted

    Every packet authenticated with XChaCha20-Poly1305.

  • Holds up on Wi-Fi

    Error correction, retransmission and pacing keep the picture steady.

  • Sound included

    Opus audio travels with the picture.
